# Brand artwork sources

Prepared on 2026-10-31. These marks identify the brands serviced; they do not imply an authorized partnership. Trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Original vector artwork was rasterized with Sharp to lossless transparent WebP (maximum 640 × 180 px), with empty padding trimmed. No brand artwork was redrawn. All files are served locally; visitors do not contact the source websites.
